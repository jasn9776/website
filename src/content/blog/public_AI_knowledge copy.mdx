## Introduction

We may be on the cusp of something big. Anthropic has announced that RSI (recursive self improvement) may be imminent. Should we trust them or is it just marketing hype?
I'm not going to present an answer; i'll just present the facts and if so maybe my doom video watching will pay off as you form your own opinion.

What is the strongest case for it being marketing hype?
Well Anthropic is preparing for an IPO, one where they hope to raise more than 100 billion dollars. Stating that they are on the verge of automating AI research would certainly boost
 their value as seen by shareholders. They want to project an image that they are the leaders of the field. 

What is the strongest case that they are serious?
Well the AI is genuinely improving and fast on external benchmarks. It seems feasible that their 80% figure is real, that indeed 80% of their code is written by AI. Recent evidence supports 90%.


## Conclusion

I think we need to admit that as a society, we really don't know how this will turn out and that it is better to be safe than sorry. 
I know there are pressures on AI companies to beat not just each other, but other countries. But ultimately, such a transformative technology 
requires very careful attention.
