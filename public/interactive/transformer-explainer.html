Honest footnotes — where this picture simplifies

  1. Two layers are drawn; GPT-2 small has 12 and frontier models have far more. The repeat marker stands in for the rest.
  2. Words are split naively here. Real models use subword tokenization (BPE), so “transformer” may become “transform” + “er”, and a beginning-of-sequence token is usually prepended.
  3. The attention crossings show one illustrative pattern. In reality every layer has many heads (12 in GPT-2 small), each with its own full pattern, all running at once.
  4. LayerNorm steps (before attention, before the MLP, and before scoring) are omitted from the graphic — they rescale each lane's vector but move no information between lanes.
  5. All numbers (768, 12, 50,257, the percentages) are for GPT-2 small and this example; they are architecture choices, not laws.
  6. Modern architectures vary: grouped-query attention shares keys/values across heads, mixture-of-experts models route between many MLPs, and some interleave local and global attention.
  7. Level 5's circuit stories (induction heads, IOI) are real published findings, but mechanistic explanations of large models remain partial — treat any single clean story with healthy suspicion.
